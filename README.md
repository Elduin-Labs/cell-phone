# Cell Phone

Real cell phones in Minecraft. Carry a phone and the villagers will call you.

Pick up, and they talk to you the only way villagers can — *hmm, hrrm, hmmh* —
using Minecraft's own villager sounds. The phone screen shows the hmms, and
right underneath, what all those hmms actually mean in real life.

## How to play

1. **Get a phone.** Craft one, or take it from the Tools tab in creative.

   | | |
   |---|---|
   | Glass Pane | Glass Pane |
   | Redstone | Redstone |
   | Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot |

2. **Keep it in your inventory.** About 20 seconds later it rings. After that,
   villagers call every few minutes.
3. **Right-click the phone and press Answer.** If you don't, it counts as a
   missed call.

## Who calls you

The villager calling is a real one near you, if there is one. Every job has
its own calls:

- The **Farmer** says something is eating the carrots. A very big rabbit.
- The **Librarian** wants to sell you a Mending book for forty emeralds.
- The **Fisherman** caught a fish *this* big. No, bigger.
- The **Mason** built a wall. It is a very good wall.
- The **Nitwit** thinks you are the pizza place.
- **Kid villagers** make prank calls.

Some villagers ask you something, and you answer **Yes** or **No**. What they
say next depends on your answer.

## Missed a call?

Right-click the phone when nobody is calling to see your recent calls.
Press **Call** next to someone to call them back.
