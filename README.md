# Cell Phone

Real cell phones in Minecraft. Carry a phone and the villagers will call you.
Pick up, and they talk to you the only way villagers can — *hmm, hrrm, hmmh* —
while the phone screen shows what all those hmms actually mean in real life.
Sometimes they ask you something, and you answer in villager too.

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.2. Needs Fabric API.

Made by Elduin.
