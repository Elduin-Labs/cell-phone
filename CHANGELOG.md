## 1.0.0

- A cell phone you can craft or grab from the Tools tab
- Villagers call you while you carry it
- They talk in real villager sounds, and the phone shows what the words mean
- Some of them ask you a question, and you answer Yes or No
- Missed a call? Open the phone and call them back
